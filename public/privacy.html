CRYPTII / PRIVACY

Privacy by default

This self-hosted cryptii deployment performs conversion, encoding, encryption, and viewing in your browser.

Your data

Input and output remain in the page unless you deliberately export a pipe file. Pipe sharing is disabled, and this deployment does not provide accounts or a database.

Local features

The app may use browser session storage while importing a pipe and a service worker to cache the application shell for offline use. These stay on your device.

Third-party services

No analytics, advertising, or third-party font resources are included. Vercel hosts the static site and may process normal web-server request metadata under its own policies.
